Compliance and cybersecurity

How MiaPLC is getting ready for the European rules on connected products. Last updated: 10 November 2026.

🚧 Draft. No MiaPLC board is on sale yet and no EU declaration of conformity has been issued. This page says which rules apply to which boards and where we stand; it will be updated at every step.

In short

  • MiaPLC-branded products will be ESP32-P4 boards: the PLC and the HMI panel. The first ones will have no WiFi; WiFi will be an option.
  • Other boards (ESP32, C3, S2, development boards, Arduino with µLink) stay for development, testing and hobby use: the free firmware runs on them, but they will not become MiaPLC products.
  • MiaPLC is not a safety device: do not use it for safety functions of machines or plants (emergency stops, light curtains, protection of people).

What the firmware already does

For the PLC, the µLink I/O modules and the panel, from the November 2026 versions (MiaPlc-idf 0.3.90, MiaHmi-idf 0.1.26). Details are in the wiki.

  • Mandatory password: every board asks for the administrator password, which you choose when you commission it. A board cannot be used without a password.
  • No identical factory passwords: the recovery password and the PLC's WiFi password are different on every board.
  • Signed commands: every request from the editor and the panel carries a signature tied to the session; a copied or altered request is rejected.
  • Passwords never on the network: challenge login; the board only keeps a verifier, not the password. Wrong attempts are slowed down.
  • Signed updates: the board only accepts firmware and editor files signed with your configuration's key.
  • Protected µLink: frames between the PLC and the I/O modules are authenticated (CMAC) and cannot be replayed.
  • HTTPS on the P4 panel, with your configuration's certificate. On the P4 PLC it is being prepared.
  • Security log: logins, rejections and bad signatures, in Diagnostics.

Boards for development and hobby use

On boards without PSRAM or smaller than the S3 and P4 the firmware protects integrity (password, signatures) but not the confidentiality of data on the network: there are not enough resources for TLS encryption. That is why they must be used only on an isolated local network, never connected to the internet, for testing and debugging. Whoever builds and uses them is responsible for them.

Installing safely

  • Put PLCs, modules and panels on a separate plant network; for remote control use a VPN, do not open router ports towards the PLC.
  • Mount the boards in a closed cabinet: whoever holds the board can reinstall it over USB.
  • Keep a copy of the configuration's credentials file (access.miapwd) and do not share it.
  • Update the firmware when a security fix is released.

Reporting a vulnerability

Write to security@miaplc.it with the product, the firmware version (Diagnostics → versions), what happens and how to reproduce it. Please do not publish the details before the fix is out.

  • Acknowledgement within 3 working days; assessment within 10.
  • Fix as soon as possible; for serious vulnerabilities the target is 30 days. Then we publish it here and in the release notes, with your name if you wish.
  • A vulnerability that is already being exploited is reported to the authorities as the Cyber Resilience Act requires.

Support and updates

Every MiaPLC board sold will have a declared support period, with its end date. Security updates will be free for the whole period.
