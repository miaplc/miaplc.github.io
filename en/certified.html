MIAplc Certified

MIAplc in plants: same software, certified boards. Last update: 13 November 2026.

🚧 Being prepared. No certified board is on sale today and the programme is not active yet. This page says how it will work.

The same software as everyone

Firmware and editor are the same ones schools and hobbyists use: free software (GPL-3.0), with no locked features and no keys to buy.

What it adds

  • CE-marked boards with an EU declaration of conformity: the MIAplc boards on ESP32-P4 and those of partner makers.
  • Compliance documentation: instructions, technical data, warnings.
  • Security updates for the whole declared support period of the board.
  • Support under a contract, for those who install and maintain plants.

When you need it

When MIAplc works in a business or in a customer's plant. Development boards remain the right way to learn and test, but their compliance is the responsibility of whoever builds them: see Compliance.

What it is not

MIAplc is not a safety device, not even on certified boards: emergency stops, light curtains and protection of people stay on a separate, certified safety circuit.

Contact

If you have a plant in mind write to info@miaplc.it.
