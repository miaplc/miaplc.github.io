Examples

Example projects

Ready-made programs to open and watch at work: from the simplest, a motor that starts and stops, to the biggest, a fish farm.

They open in the simulator, in your browser, with no board. Inside the simulator you also find them under 📂 Open. Got a board? Download the .miaplc file and open it in the board's editor.

Complete example · PLC + 2 µLink modules + HMI panel

Belacqua: 100 sturgeons in 20 tanks

A complete farm, with the water flowing in a closed loop. The PLC controls oxygen and feeding tank by tank and runs the pumps slowly when that is enough, to save energy. The panel is industrial-style, with one page per tank.

A design example, not yet tested on a real plant. VFD pumps, 146 alarms; projects, program, panel, wiring and manual to download.

How to open an example in the simulator

  1. Openpress ▶ Open in the simulator under the example (or 📂 Open inside the simulator).
  2. Confirmif the editor asks to replace the project, answer yes.
  3. Download and RunDownload to cpu, confirm, then Run.
  4. Onlinepress Online and switch the inputs on in the MOCK PLC panel, the fake PLC at the bottom right.
Basic · 3 networks

Motor start / stop

The first program of every automation course: the self-holding circuit. Start runs the motor, its own contact keeps it on, Stop or the thermal overload (the motor protection) stop it. A lamp lights up 3 seconds after starting.

  1. Click I0 (Start) and click again to release it: Q0 stays on.
  2. After 3 s Q1 lights up.
  3. Click I1 (Stop) or I2 (overload): the motor stops.

Timers · 6 networks

Traffic light

Three cascaded timers run the cycle green 5 s, yellow 2 s, red 4 s. With the traffic light off, yellow flashes. It uses SM5, a system bit that turns on and off by itself once a second (1 Hz clock).

  1. Without touching anything: Q1 flashes.
  2. Click I0 (Enable): the cycle starts on Q0, Q1, Q2.
  3. In Online, watch the timers count inside the TON boxes.

Advanced · FC, DB, comparisons

The relay song

Relays can't play notes, but they can play rhythm: Beethoven's Fifth, Jingle Bells and “Shave and a haircut” beaten out on Q0 and Q1. A step sequencer built with a function (FC), timers and comparisons. On a real board you hear the relays click. (Comments in Italian.)

  1. After Run the music starts by itself.
  2. I0: start again from the top.
  3. Open FC10 to see how the sequencer is built.
