What project donations pay for

  • 🧩 chips, development boards and components
  • 🖨️ PCBs, prototypes and assembly
  • 📦 µLink modules for the 50, 100, 500-board tests
  • 🚚 shipping, customs and VAT on materials
  • 📟 test tools: oscilloscope, logic analyser, power supply
  • 🧰 support tools: software, documentation, technical standards
  • 🌐 domain, website, email and servers when needed

My time, which stays voluntary, and no personal expenses. That's what the coffee is for.

🚧 under construction Anonymous diagnostics from the editor

Soon, from the editor's advanced diagnostics, you will be able to send us your board's statistics: hardware configuration, cycle times, RAM and stack usage, memory used, active modules, timings per FC. They help us understand how MiaPLC behaves on real plants and where to improve it.

Before sending you see the data and you confirm. No programs, variable names or personal data are sent, and the board never connects to the internet: the editor sends the data, only when you decide to.

⚠ Statistics collection is enabled in MiaConfig and is meant for bench testing: it slows the PLC down and can cause lock-ups with a board restart. Do not use it on running machines or plants.
