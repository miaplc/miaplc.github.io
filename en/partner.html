Board makers

Partner

For those who make boards and want to use the MIAplc name. Last update: 13 November 2026.

🚧 Being prepared. The programme for board makers is not active yet and its terms are still to be defined.

The software is free, the name is not

Anyone may run MIAplc on their own board, without asking and without paying: the GPL-3.0 allows it. The board and its advertising may say "compatible with MIAplc". Using the name and the logo as part of the product needs an agreement: see Trademark.

What the programme includes

  • Compatibility tests of the board with the firmware and with MiaConfig.
  • Use of "MIAplc Certified" and of the logo on the board, the packaging and the website.
  • The board in the list of certified ones, on this site.
  • A direct channel for security fixes.

In return: a written agreement and a fee per board. The CE marking and the compliance of the board remain the maker's.

Contact

If you make boards and are interested write to info@miaplc.it.
