The MIAplc trademark

How the MIAplc name may be used. Last update: 13 November 2026.

MIAplc™ is a trademark filed in Italy by Denys Momotenko (UIBM application no. 302026000194101 of 9 November 2026), registration pending. The software is free (GPL-3.0). The name and the logo are not: they tell people what comes from the project.

Allowed, without asking

  • Saying that a product or a project is "compatible with MIAplc" or "based on MIAplc", when it is true.
  • Using the name to talk about it, teach it, review it, write about it.
  • Publishing a modified version of the software, as the GPL allows, under another name and saying it derives from MIAplc.

Needs a written agreement

  • Calling a product, a board, a company, a domain or a social profile MIAplc, or a name that looks like it.
  • Using the logo on products, packaging or advertising.
  • Writing "MIAplc Certified", "official" or "approved by MIAplc".
  • Distributing a modified version of the software under the MIAplc name.

How to write it

MIAplc: MIA in capitals, plc in lower case. MIA stands for Micro Integrated Automation.

Other trademarks

Third-party product names and trademarks mentioned on this site belong to their respective owners. MIAplc is an independent project and is not connected to them.
